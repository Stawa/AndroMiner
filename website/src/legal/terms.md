## Mining can damage your phone

AndroMiner runs your phone's processor at high load for long periods. That is what mining is, and it carries real risk:

- Sustained heat can damage or shorten the life of the battery, screen, storage and mainboard.
- Charging while hot can accelerate battery aging, swelling or failure.
- A phone can slow down, shut off, crash, lose data or stop working permanently.
- Phones earn very little from mining. Electricity and wear usually cost more than the coins.

AndroMiner includes device protection that reduces load or stops mining when readings get high. It is a safety net, not a guarantee. It depends on sensors your phone reports and cannot catch every fault.

**You use AndroMiner entirely at your own risk. The AndroMiner developers and contributors are not responsible for any damage to your phone, battery, accessories or data, or for any other loss or harm that results from installing or using it.**

## Use a spare device

Mine only on a device you can afford to lose, never on your daily phone or one that holds important data. Back up anything you care about first. Keep the phone on a flat, ventilated surface, start with low settings, stay nearby while it runs, and stop if the case gets hot.
